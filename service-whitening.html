Teeth whitening

Whitening lightens the natural colour of your teeth. It works well for many people, but it has limits — here is what it can and cannot do, and how to make the result last.

Ask on WhatsApp Common questions

At a glance

  • Suits Yellowing or staining of natural teeth, with healthy gums and no untreated decay
  • Options In-clinic whitening, or custom trays with gel to use at home
  • Common effect Temporary sensitivity for a day or two
  • How long it lasts Months to a few years, depending on coffee, tea and smoking

Typical ranges only — your examination decides what applies to you.

Worth knowing first

  • Fillings, crowns and veneers do not change colour. They may need replacing afterwards to match.
  • Some discolouration, such as certain grey or medication-related stains, responds poorly to whitening.
  • It is usually postponed during pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Products bought online can contain unregulated ingredients. Whitening under a dentist's supervision is the safer route.

What happens, step by step

Every visit starts with an explanation. Nothing begins until you have agreed to it.

01

Check first

We check for decay, gum problems and old fillings or crowns, and note your current shade.

02

Clean

A scale and polish removes surface stains, so the whitening works on the tooth itself.

03

Whitening

In clinic, the gums are protected and the gel applied in timed rounds. For home whitening, trays are made to fit your teeth.

04

Review

We compare the new shade and talk through how to keep it.

Common questions

Does whitening damage enamel?

Whitening done under a dentist's supervision, with suitable products, is generally considered safe for enamel. Overuse can cause problems, which is one reason to have it supervised.

Will my teeth be sensitive?

Some people have short-lived sensitivity. It usually settles within a day or two; we can adjust the approach if it bothers you.

How white will they get?

It varies from person to person and cannot be promised in advance. We give you a realistic idea after seeing your teeth.

How do I keep the result?

Cut down on staining drinks and smoking, keep up regular cleaning, and consider occasional top-ups.
