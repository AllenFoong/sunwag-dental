Restorative

Implants

Most people picture an implant as one tooth being planted. It is three separate pieces, fitted over more than one visit, with healing time in between. Drag the model to look around it.

  1. 1

    Crown

    The part you see and chew with. Shaped and shaded to match the teeth beside it, and fitted last.

  2. 2

    Abutment

    The connector in the middle. It locks the crown onto the post, and goes in once the gum has healed.

  3. 3

    Implant Post

    A titanium screw that replaces the root in the jawbone. Bone needs a few months to grow onto it and hold it firm.

Orthodontics

Clear aligners (Invisalign)

Clear, removable trays swapped in stages to move teeth gradually. They only work if worn around 20–22 hours a day, so we first check whether your case suits aligners and whether they fit your routine — then you decide.

Oral surgery

Root canal treatment

For a tooth whose nerve is inflamed or has died. The affected pulp is removed, the canals cleaned and then sealed, so the tooth itself can be kept. We X-ray first to confirm the situation, then explain roughly how many visits it will take.

Everyday and other care

This is what most people come in for.

Check-up & scaling

Removing tartar and stains, and checking for anything starting to go wrong. Every six months is a good rhythm — a cavity caught early is far simpler than one that has become a toothache.

Toothache & urgent visits

Sudden pain, a chipped tooth, a filling that came out — message us with the details and we will see whether we can fit you in the same day.

Dentures and Crowns

For damaged or missing teeth. We explain which approaches apply to your situation and how they differ.
