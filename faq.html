Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

A message first is better. Tell us what you would like looked at and roughly when suits you, and we will hold a slot — rather than you arriving to find a wait.

For sudden pain or an emergency, message us anyway and we will see whether we can make room the same day.

What is the fastest way to book?

WhatsApp 012-352 8627. You can send a message at any hour and we will reply when we see it.

If typing is inconvenient, call the same number.

What are the opening hours?

Hours shift around public holidays and the occasional unexpected day, so please treat WhatsApp as the source of truth rather than risk a wasted trip.

"What time are you open today?" is enough.

What should I bring the first time?

Identification is enough. If you have had X-rays or treatment elsewhere, or you take medication regularly, tell us — it changes how we proceed.

Where are you, and is parking easy?

36 & 38, Jalan Pendekar 13, Taman Ungku Tun Aminah, 81300 Johor Bahru. There are parking spaces directly in front of the shop.

The map and directions are on the contact page.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the situation — the same tooth can be treated in several ways at different costs. After the examination we tell you what is needed and roughly what it comes to, and nothing starts until you agree.

If you would like a rough idea beforehand, describe it on WhatsApp and we will try to give you a range.

Can I bring my child?

Yes. Message us with their age and what needs looking at, and we will pick a suitable time — a child's first dental visit goes better when the clinic is quiet.

I am nervous about dentists. Can we just talk first?

Yes, and it usually makes things easier. Tell us on WhatsApp what worries you. You can also come in for the examination alone, hear what we found, and decide afterwards whether to go further.

Does my tooth have to come out? Do I need braces?

Honestly, we have to see it. Answering that over the internet would not be responsible — two wisdom teeth can sit at different angles, one pressing on its neighbour and one not, and the answer changes completely.

Come in for an examination. Once we can see it properly we will lay out the options and the differences between them, and you decide.

Something we have not covered?

Just ask. No need to be shy about it.
