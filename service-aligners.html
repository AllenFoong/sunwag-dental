Clear aligners (Invisalign)

A series of clear, removable trays, each nudging the teeth a little further. They are easy to live with — but they only work if you wear them. Here is what to expect.

Ask on WhatsApp Common questions

At a glance

  • Suits Mild to moderate crowding, gaps and some bite issues — the exam confirms
  • Wear time About 20–22 hours a day; out only to eat, drink anything but water, and clean
  • Trays A new set typically every 1–2 weeks
  • Duration From several months to around two years, plus a retainer afterwards

Typical ranges only — your examination decides what applies to you.

Worth knowing first

  • Wear time is the whole treatment. Trays left in a drawer do not move teeth, and the plan falls behind.
  • Take them out to eat and for any drink except plain water, and brush before putting them back in.
  • Each new tray usually feels tight for a day or two.
  • Not every case suits aligners. If braces would do a clearly better job, we will say so.

What happens, step by step

Every visit starts with an explanation. Nothing begins until you have agreed to it.

01

Assessment

We check whether your teeth and bite suit aligners, and take photos, X-rays and a scan or impressions.

02

Your plan

A digital plan shows the planned movement step by step and roughly how many trays you will need.

03

Wearing the trays

Small tooth-coloured bumps (attachments) may be added to help the trays grip. You switch trays at home on schedule.

04

Check-ups and retainer

Periodic visits to check progress. Extra trays are sometimes needed to fine-tune, then a retainer holds the result.

Common questions

Can people see them?

They are clear, so they are hard to notice at a normal speaking distance. Attachments, if used, are tooth-coloured.

What if I lose or break a tray?

Message us. Often you can move on to the next tray or go back to the previous one until a replacement is sorted.

Is it faster than braces?

Not necessarily. It depends on how much movement is needed and, with aligners, on how consistently they are worn.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel pressure or mild soreness for a day or two after switching trays, which settles quickly.
