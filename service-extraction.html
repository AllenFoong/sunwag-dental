Tooth extraction

Sometimes a tooth cannot be saved, or is causing more trouble than it is worth. Here is how an extraction is done, what to do in the first few days afterwards and when to call us.

Ask on WhatsApp Common questions

At a glance

  • Common reasons Deep decay, a cracked tooth, advanced gum disease, crowding, or a problem wisdom tooth
  • Anaesthetic Local anaesthetic
  • Healing The gum usually closes over in 1–2 weeks; bone fills in over a few months
  • Afterwards We discuss whether the gap should be filled

Typical ranges only — your examination decides what applies to you.

Worth knowing first

  • First 24 hours: no rinsing, spitting or drinking through a straw, so the blood clot stays in place.
  • Avoid smoking for as long as you can — it is a major cause of dry socket, a painful delayed-healing problem.
  • Eat soft food on the other side, and from the next day rinse gently with warm salt water after meals.
  • Contact us if bleeding will not stop, pain gets worse after day three, swelling keeps growing or you develop a fever.

What happens, step by step

Every visit starts with an explanation. Nothing begins until you have agreed to it.

01

Assessment

We examine the tooth and take an X-ray to see the roots. Tell us about any medication, especially blood thinners.

02

Numbing

Local anaesthetic numbs the area. You feel pressure, not sharp pain.

03

Removing the tooth

The tooth is loosened and removed. Some teeth, such as impacted wisdom teeth, need a small surgical procedure, and may be referred.

04

Aftercare

You bite on gauze to stop the bleeding and go home with written aftercare instructions.

Common questions

Does wisdom tooth removal work the same way?

It can be straightforward, but impacted wisdom teeth often need surgery and a longer recovery. It depends on the angle and position seen on the X-ray.

Can I go back to work the same day?

Many people return to desk work the next day. Avoid strenuous exercise for a day or two.

Do I need to replace the tooth?

Not always, for example with wisdom teeth. For other teeth, a gap can let neighbours drift; options include an implant, bridge or denture.

How long does the numbness last?

Usually a few hours. Avoid hot drinks and chewing until it wears off so you do not bite your cheek.
