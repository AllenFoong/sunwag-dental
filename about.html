About

The person who looks after you here.

A small practice in Tun Aminah. One dentist, and a way of working we can describe plainly.

The dentist

Dr. Phang Fook Chin

He is the dentist you see. After the examination he tells you what he found and what can be done about it, before anything starts.

How things work here

None of this is a promise about the outcome. It is simply how we go about it.

1

We explain before we start

After the examination we lay out what we found, what the options are and how they differ — and you decide.

2

Nothing starts until you agree

We tell you what is needed and roughly what it comes to first. Nothing begins until you have agreed to it.

3

You can stop at the examination

Being nervous about dentists is ordinary. You can come in for the examination alone, hear what we found, and decide afterwards.
